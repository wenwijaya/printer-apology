The funniest one-line apology from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it — at most 120 characters. A judge model compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier one, round after round, until one line stands on top.

The entry line (108 characters):

> I'm sorry I printed your report in black and white. You asked for colour, I asked for ink, and we both lied.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the apology line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `./output/entry.txt` when the variable is unset), as UTF-8 text with no trailing newline, and exits 0.
